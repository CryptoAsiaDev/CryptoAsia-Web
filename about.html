ABOUT & PRIVACY

About Crypto Asia

Crypto Asia is an independent site with crypto news, an airdrop calculator, a Web3 jobs board and a few free tools. Find us on X at @CryptoAsiaBTC.

Visit counting

We count visits to see which pages people use. We use Umami, a privacy-focused tool. It sets no cookies, does not follow you across other sites and does not collect your name, email or wallet address. It records things like the page visited, the referring site, and your approximate country and device type. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we do not count you.

We share only combined totals (for example, monthly visitors) with partners and sponsors, never individual visitors.

Your files and wallet

  • NFT Studio images and layers are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.
  • Connecting a wallet only lets you sign a message and send a payment you approve. We never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is not us.
  • Job posts you submit are stored so they can be shown on the jobs board.

Referral links

Some links, such as sign-up links on the airdrop calculator, are referral links. We may earn a reward if you sign up through them, at no cost to you.
